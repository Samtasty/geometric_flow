Table~\ref{tab:train_test_behavior_first_last} shows that the logging policy improves over rounds on all four datasets. The held-out conclusions remain largely unchanged after the fix, but the comparison to behavior is now cleaner. On \texttt{attempts}, \texttt{skill\_builder}, and \texttt{PIX}, the learned policies still improve substantially over behavior on the test split. On \texttt{attempts}, DR remains the strongest balanced policy, with the best test DR ($2.570$) and DM ($2.307$), both above behavior. On \texttt{skill\_builder}, the three learned policies are close, but DR again has the strongest DM value. On \texttt{PIX}, the matched-objective pattern remains clear: IPS is best on IPS, SNIPS on SNIPS, DR on DR and DM, and MIS on MIS.

\texttt{Assistments8000} remains the weak case. The learned policies are only slightly above behavior under IPS/SNIPS/DR, and all of them remain below the oracle under DM. This is still the dataset where the evidence for policy improvement is least robust.
